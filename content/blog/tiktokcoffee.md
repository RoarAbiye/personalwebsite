Sitting at a local coffee shop that is very small about five chairs. It’s a quiet place, but there is already a TikTok enjoyer disturbing my peace. It’s funny how the TikTok noise has become background noise now, like an ASMR sound: the laughs and short sounds cycling, looping. It has faded into the background. What does this mean if we try to get artsy-fartsy and analytical about it? We have learned (or so I think) how this short-form content is hurting our attention spans and all that. But now it’s in the background our constant, damaging hit to our brainpower is becoming the background of our lives. Our brain damage constitutes-or is-the soundtrack of our existence.
​I remember Žižek saying we need to bring back shame; as in, we should start to feel shame for our misbehaviors. It’s great. People shouldn't brag about being corrupted; they should at least have the decency to hide it. It would show that they still believe in the need to be morally righteous or something. I feel like we should extend that idea to this TikTok slop phenomenon. People should at least be ashamed of consuming slop; they should at least have the decency to use earphones.
​Sometimes I feel like I’m trying to virtue signal (honestly, I am). I’m not completely in the realm of digital emancipation. I’m still guilty of replacing slop with mobile games. I don't think I can last one minute without checking my phone. It’s like a disease. I can only work towards being unchained from these digital shackles one link at a time.

I think I'm done. Auf Wiedersehen!
